Chelmsford College website prototype

A clickable, mobile-first prototype of the four pages in the demonstration brief (homepage, course page, events and payment journey), plus search, shortlist and parent journeys. Every course page is generated from the college's supplied course data.

Version 2 applies the Chelmsford College design system: Chelmsford Blue and Green, a supporting palette, Bricolage Grotesque and Atkinson Hyperlegible type, the “swoosh” from the logo, and the college's own photography. The design lives in one stylesheet, so the greyscale structure underneath is unchanged.

Version 2: designed 29 October 2026 Status: draft for internal review Prepared by ClerksWell

Demo journeys

The site itself carries no on-screen guidance, so the presenter drives every journey from memory. The steps below and the separate demo script are the presenter's notes. Present journeys 1 and 2 on a phone (or a narrow window) first, then show the same pages in the responsive viewer.

How to review this

  • Annotations are off by default. Use the “Show annotations” button at the bottom right of any page. Yellow notes explain behaviour, data and CMS controls; red notes are questions for the college. On course pages, annotations also label each element with its SQL column.

  • Search, filters, accordions, the fee checker, shortlist, event booking and the whole payment journey work. Links to pages outside the brief (news, apprenticeships, about us) go nowhere by design.

  • Course content is the college's own demo data. Events, payment references and student stories are realistic placeholders.
