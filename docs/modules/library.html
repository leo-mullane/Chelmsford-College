    What do the levels mean?

    Every qualification has a level. The higher the level, the more advanced it is. You can move up a level each year.

    LevelRoughly the same as
    Entry level and supportedBuilding skills and confidence before Level 1
    Level 1GCSEs at grades 3 to 1
    Level 2GCSEs at grades 9 to 4
    Level 3A Levels and T Levels: can lead to university
    Level 4 and aboveThe first year of a degree (HTQs, HNCs)

    Not sure which level is right for you? Call our careers team on 01245 293031.
