Chelmsford College website prototype

A clickable, mobile-first prototype of the four pages in the demonstration brief (homepage, course page, events and payment journey), plus search, shortlist and parent journeys. Every course page is generated from the college's supplied course data.

Demo journeys

The site itself carries no on-screen guidance, so the presenter drives every journey from memory. The steps below and the separate demo script are the presenter's notes. The demonstration is remote, so share a narrow browser window for the mobile view of journeys 1 and 2 first, then show the same pages side by side in the responsive viewer.

About 6 minutes · mobile

1. Maya, 16, finds a course and books the Showcase Open Evening

  1. Homepage campaign and course search, then “I'm 16–18”
  2. Search “fashion” and filter to Level 3
  3. Course page from SQL: level explainer, fees checker, save
  4. Shortlist: share with a parent, compare
  5. Showcase Open Evening: presentations by campus, then book an arrival time for two and add it to the calendar

Page templates

  • Homepage R10 R11 R12 R30 R31 R43 R44

    Campaign hero, course search with “who are you” routes and career search, subjects, video, events, news.

  • Course search results R13 R33

    Filter by who it's for, subject, level, study mode and campus, with live counts, a mobile drawer and shareable URLs.

  • Course page (one template, 33 courses) R14 R15 R16 R35–R39

    Rendered from Appendix 2: key facts, accordions, fee checker, sticky Apply on mobile. Turn on annotations to see the SQL column behind each element.

  • Events listing R17 R40

    Filter by type, audience and campus, in a list or calendar view.

  • Single event and booking R18 R41

    The college's Showcase Open Evening: date, time, location, description, presentations by campus, parking and help, then booking with an arrival time, validation, confirmation and add to calendar. Fully booked example.

  • Payment journey R19–R23 R42

    Deposits, materials fees and trips. Students or parents and carers pay without an account, and the card is taken on the provider's hosted page.

  • Shortlist and compare R25

    Save courses, compare up to 3, and share with a parent or carer.

  • Apply hand-off R26

    The course is carried through from the course page. This is the conversion point the college measures.

  • Parents and carers hub R24

    Page outline for a priority audience that is currently buried.

Beyond the brief

  • Course matcher R60

    Four questions, scored against the course data, with a reason for every match.

  • Subject pathway page R61

    Every subject's route from Level 1 to university or work, built from the course data. Construction, Business.

  • Employers and apprenticeship vacancies R62

    An employer hub with a three-step vacancy form for a priority audience.

  • Application tracker R63

    Status after applying, interview booking and parent or carer updates.

  • Display settings R64

    Text size, contrast, reading spacing, link underlines and reduced motion, from the audience bar on any page.

  • Ask about this course R65

    Instant answers from the course record, with a hand-off to admissions.

  • Site-wide search R66

    The Search button in the header opens an overlay with live results grouped by type. Press Enter for the full results page, with type filters, counts, best bets and “Did you mean”.

How to review this

  • Annotations are off by default. Use the “Show annotations” button at the bottom right of any page. Yellow notes explain behaviour, data and CMS controls; red notes are questions for the college. On course pages, annotations also label each element with its SQL column.

  • Search, filters, accordions, the fee checker, shortlist, event booking and the whole payment journey work. Links to pages outside the brief (news, apprenticeships, about us) go nowhere by design.

  • Course content is the college's own demo data, and the Showcase Open Evening is the college's own sample event. The other events, payment references and student stories are realistic placeholders.
