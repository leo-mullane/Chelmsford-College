BehaviourThe link to this page arrives by email as soon as someone applies, so there is no account to create. It shows where the application is, what happens next and the one thing the applicant needs to do now. Most applications are lost between applying and the interview, which is exactly when the current site goes quiet.

IntegrationStatus and interview slots come from the college's admissions system. Booking a slot writes back to it, and reminders go out by email and text. Parents and carers can be added to receive the same updates, which matters for under-18s.
