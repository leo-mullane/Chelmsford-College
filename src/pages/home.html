NavigationEvery top-level item opens a mega menu with second-tier links and one promoted card. Editors choose the card's image, text and link per menu, and can schedule it like the campaign hero. The Courses menu has audience tabs (hover, click or arrow keys) and a live search that returns courses, careers and subjects as you type, straight from the course data. On mobile the menus drill down inside the menu drawer, with a “Main menu” back link. Escape closes any menu and returns focus to its button.

CMSOne campaign at a time, scheduled by marketing with start and end dates (for example Open Evening, then “Applications open”, then Results Day). There is no auto-rotating carousel: the current site rotates 13 slides, so only the first is ever seen, and auto-rotation fails WCAG 2.2.2 without a pause control. Up to three secondary messages sit as cards further down the page.

    BehaviourType two letters to see suggestions grouped as Careers, Subjects and Courses (try “nu” or “fash”). Use the arrow keys to move through them and Escape to close. “I want to be a…” switches to a career search, which matches jobs to courses using a career taxonomy seeded from each course's C_EMPLOYABILITY text. The quick routes filter by who you are, which is how families think, rather than by college department.
