Not sure what to study?

Answer four quick questions and we'll suggest courses that fit what you enjoy and where you are now. It takes about a minute.

BehaviourOne question per screen, with a progress bar and Back. Answers are scored against the course data: interests map to course_sections, expected grades map to level via course_level_link, and stage (school leaver, adult, supported) maps to the age groups. Each result says why it matches. Nothing is stored or sent, and the results can be saved to the shortlist in one tap.

CMSThe interest options and the subjects they point to are editable by marketing, so a new subject area can be added to the matcher without a developer.
