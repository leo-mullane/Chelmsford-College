Search the site

BehaviourOne search box covers the whole site: courses and subjects from the course database, events, information pages, help answers and news. Job adverts and newsletters are held in the college’s SQL tables too, so they are indexed in the same way. Results are grouped by type with live counts, and the type buttons narrow the list without reloading. A best bet sits above the results for the searches people make most (payments, term dates, open events), and is set by the marketing team in the CMS. Misspellings get a “Did you mean” suggestion. The URL updates, so a search can be shared.
