My shortlist

Save courses as you browse, compare them, and share the list with a parent or carer. No account needed.

BehaviourThe shortlist is stored on the device, with no login and no personal data. “Share” creates a link like shortlist.html?ids=374,482 that opens the same list on a parent's phone. If the learner books an event, the shortlist travels with the booking so tutors know what they are interested in.
